About Siddnath krushi kedra

Serving farmers with trust, quality and honest guidance since day one.

Who We Are

Siddnath krushi kedra provides trusted agricultural products to farmers. We supply fertilizers, pesticides, seeds and farming tools at affordable prices, sourced from reliable and licensed manufacturers so every product on our shelf meets quality standards.

We started with a simple goal — to be a shop farmers can walk into and get honest advice, not just a sale. Over time, that approach has helped us build lasting relationships with the farming community we serve.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve crop yield and support farmers with quality products and guidance. We believe better farming starts with better inputs and the right knowledge to use them effectively.

Whether it's choosing the right fertilizer for your soil type or picking a pesticide safe for a specific crop, our team is here to help you make informed decisions season after season.
